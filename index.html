OVERVIEW

What is
Venture Surety?
(Vensurety)

At many stages of development, startups are asked to post collateral as a backstop to contractual obligations. This may be for supplier contracts, customers, utilities, or even investors.

Surety bonds are a common off-balance sheet alternative to posting collateral. To date, surety has either not been available to emerging climate tech companies or is available and very expensive. There are many instances where these companies could use surety to guarantee their payment or performance instead of cash collateral or a letter of credit. GreenieRE and Trellis launched Vensurety in fall 2025 to provide surety bonds to climate tech companies struggling with trapped capital.

WHAT IS SURETY?

Surety is a three-party contract.

1 / SURETY

The bond provider.

2 / PRINCIPAL

The party performing the work and agreeing to indemnify the surety.

3 / OBLIGEE

The party requiring and protected by the bond, and defining the obligation.

Where does a broker fit?

Brokers are not part of the surety agreement, but can help a company figure out what bond type they need and put together a submission package.

Key provisions

General Indemnity Agreement

A separate agreement in which the Principal agrees to reimburse (indemnify) the Surety for all losses from the bond. This is the Surety’s primary protection and how bonds differ from insurance.

Term

How long the bond remains in force. Usually annually or the duration of the contract.

Rate

Cost of the bond. A percentage of the bond penalty amount.

Bond amount / penal sum

The maximum amount paid for not fulfilling the obligation.

GETTING A BOND

How and when to get a bond.

Steps in getting a bond

Step1

Review contract requirements

The obligee defines the performance metric to be guaranteed and the bond amount required.

Step2

Select a surety agent / broker

The principal engages a licensed surety broker, who markets the risk to one or more surety companies and assists in completing the application. This is an optional, but helpful step. A broker is not required, but can be useful if a company is unfamiliar with surety.

Step3

Submit underwriting package

The principal prepares a file with financial and technical documents supporting the bond request.

Step4

Surety underwrites

The surety evaluates the bond and determines rate and collateral required.

Step5

Execute indemnity agreement

Prior to bond issuance, the principal (and any required indemnitors) executes the General Indemnity Agreement.

Step6

Bond issued; premium paid

The surety executes the bond and the principal pays the premium. The bond is attached to or incorporated into the underlying contract.

What is an indemnity agreement?

The contract in which the principal agrees to reimburse the surety company for any losses it pays out under the bond. Think of it as the credit backstop that makes the surety willing to issue the bond in the first place.
